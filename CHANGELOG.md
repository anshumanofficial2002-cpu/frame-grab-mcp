# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-11

First release.

- `grab_frame`, `sample_frames` and `contact_sheet` return frames as MCP image content
- `detect_scenes` with shot stats, scene sheet and CSV
- `make_thumbnail` with auto-pick (sharpness, contrast, exposure, color) and 9 platform presets
- `extract_frames` (count, interval, scenes, timestamps) and `make_preview` (GIF, MP4, WebP)
